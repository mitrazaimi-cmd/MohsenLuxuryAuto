Balance: about 70% black, 22% silver, 8% gold. Never large gold areas or long gold text. On gold buttons, the text is black (#000 on #D9AE5F ≈ 10:1).

## Typography

- Headings: **Cinzel** 400–600, uppercase, letter spacing 0.06–0.12em
- Body: **Montserrat** 300–500, 16px base on the web, line height 1.6–1.7
- Eyebrow / caption: Montserrat 500, 11–12px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.28em, gold
- Load with `next/font/google` (no layout shift)

## Spacing (spacious)

4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128 px. Section padding: 96px desktop, 64px mobile. Content max width: 1200px. Side gutter: 24px (mobile 20px).

## Components

- **Primary button:** 3D gold (the owner asked for raised, not flat, buttons on 2026-10-20): a metallic gold gradient lit from above with a bright top edge, a 3px bronze lip below, a soft shadow and a warm gold glow; black text, uppercase Montserrat 600, letter spacing 0.18em, 2px radius, min height 48px. Hover lifts it 1px with a stronger glow; pressing sinks it onto its lip. Visible focus ring (2px gold outline, 3px offset).
- **Secondary button:** the same 3D treatment in dark: a warm bronze-black gradient face with a light top edge, gold border and light-gold text, a 3px bronze lip and a faint gold glow. Clearly raised, but the gold primary stays the one that stands out.
- **Card:** surface background, 1px line border, optional 1px gold top hairline; hover: border turns gold-soft (no layout shift).
- **Input:** surface-2 background, 1px line border, 16px text, visible label above, error text below the field in `--danger`.
- **Section header:** gold eyebrow, Cinzel title, short 48px gold hairline.
- **Centered heading** (the owner's name for it, «عنوان وسط»): the section header with its eyebrow, title, hairline and intro centered on the page, via `<SectionHead center>`. Used for About "Experience" and "Mission", Portfolio "Reviews", and homepage "Your consultation".
- **Wide photo** (the owner's name for it, «عکس عریض»): a cinematic photo across the full container width, framed with gold corners, between a centered heading and the content below: `.frame.corners.photo-wide` (21:9, 16:9 on phones) with `sizes="(max-width: 1260px) 100vw, 1200px"`. Used for homepage "Your consultation", where `.check-list.inline` also puts the short check marks in one centered row.
- **Gold boxes** (the owner's name for it): a list where each item is its own box that fills with a soft gold wash on hover, its border turns gold and its text and icons brighten. Usually laid out side by side. Markup: `.steps.boxed` (numbered) or `.check-list.boxed` (check marks) on the list, `.gold-hover` on each item, and `.grid-3` / `.grid-4` for the row (2 per row on tablets, 1 on phones). `.gold-hover` alone works on any box, such as a `.card`. Used for: homepage "Your consultation", Services "What your consultation includes" and "What to bring", About "Experience", the "Top salesperson" / "3 languages" cards and the Mission values (Integrity, Customer satisfaction, Best value). A `.card.gold-top` can't take `.gold-hover` (both use ::before), so drop `gold-top` on gold boxes.
- **Photo cards:** `.spec-card` (Services "Specialties"): a photo with the name at the bottom; the description opens on hover under the same gold wash, and always shows on touch screens.
- **Heading beside a photo:** `.split.intro-split` with the section header and a `.frame.corners` photo (`.photo-3x2` or `.photo-6x5`); stacks below 960px.

## Motion

Fade-up 12px over 350ms, ease-out, on viewport enter. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, show everything in its final state. Hover transitions 200ms. Nothing fast or flashy.

One exception, the owner's decision: the homepage hero's animated logo (`components/hero-logo.tsx`) plays for everyone, reduced motion included. It plays once, about 5 seconds, then holds on the still logo. Windows reports reduced motion whenever its animation effects are turned off, which hid the logo animation from many laptop visitors.

## Page pattern

Hero (logo, slogan, CTA) → proof stats → key messages → services → testimonials and deliveries (social proof) → final booking CTA. A sticky header keeps "Book a Consultation" visible.

## Anti-patterns

Emoji icons · white page backgrounds · the Mercedes-Benz star · stretched or recolored logo · urgency wording ("last chance") · invisible focus states · placeholder-only labels.

Exceptions, the owner's decision (2026-10-20), made knowing the star rule and the dealership/trademark risk: three photos show the star: on a car's grille behind Mohsen in the homepage "Why clients come back" photo (`public/images/why-clients-handshake.jpg`) and the Services consultation photo (`public/images/consultation.webp`), and on the steering wheel in the homepage "Your consultation" test-drive photo (`public/images/test-drive.webp`). The About portrait (`public/images/mohsen-portrait.webp`) also shows the star and a Mercedes-Benz sign; the owner chose on 2026-10-21 to keep it for now. Don't treat any of them as license for other images.
