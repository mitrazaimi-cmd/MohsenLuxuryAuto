# Mohsen Luxury Auto Brand Guide

Oct 16, 2026

Mohsen Luxury Auto is the personal brand of a Master Certified Mercedes-Benz sales specialist with 20 years at Mercedes-Benz, built so clients can **Expect the Best**.

## Brand Foundation

### Brand Story

Mohsen has loved cars since childhood. The first time he sat behind the wheel of an S-Class, he knew he wanted to give that experience to others. Twenty years later, he is a Master Certified Mercedes-Benz sales specialist at the Westchester branch.

- Top salesperson, averaging 20 cars per month
- Highest customer satisfaction and an outstanding CSI score
- Master Certified
- 80 to 85 percent of his clients come back to buy again
- Speaks English, Farsi and some Spanish

### Mission

To put customer satisfaction first by taking the time to show every client each car patiently, advising them on what truly fits their needs, and following up on every sale.

### Vision

To grow Mohsen Luxury Auto into an independent brand, recognized by name, where more than 90 percent of clients are returning customers.

### Values

| Value | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| Integrity | Everything is disclosed up front, with no hidden costs. |
| Customer satisfaction | Every decision starts with what is best for the client. |
| Best value | The client leaves knowing they got the most for their money. |

## Target Audience

The core client is a high-income, well-educated professional or business owner, and business owners are the main group to grow.

| | Buyer persona: The Business Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Who | High-income, well-educated professionals and business owners |
| Buys | GLC and GLE, new and Certified Pre-Owned, mostly financed |
| Also served | AMG, EQ electric, Maybach, business and fleet purchases |
| Biggest worry | That something is not fully disclosed, or that there are hidden costs |
| Wants to feel | Satisfied, with a great buying experience |
| Finds Mohsen through | Referrals and word of mouth |

Because most new clients arrive by referral, every piece of content should be easy for a happy client to share.

## Positioning, USP and Tagline

### Positioning Statement

For business owners and professionals who want a Mercedes-Benz without the usual dealership stress, Mohsen Luxury Auto is the personal sales specialist who is always available, discloses everything up front, and stays with the client long after delivery.

### Unique Selling Point

One point of contact who handles everything, from the first test drive to all the paperwork to personal delivery, and who is always one call away.

### Proof Points

- 20 years with Mercedes-Benz and Master Certified
- About 20 cars sold per month
- 80 to 85 percent repeat clients
- What clients say: Mohsen always takes good care of us. He is always available, and everybody can easily get in touch with him.

### Competitive Edge

When a client is comparing with BMW, the message is the driving experience: a Mercedes-Benz drives noticeably smoother, and Mohsen knows every model well enough to show the difference.

## Verbal Identity

The brand sounds **elegant, trustworthy and warm**, like an experienced friend rather than a salesperson. All brand content is in English.

### Tone by Situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social posts | Friendly and short | Delivery day for Dr. Patel. Enjoy every mile of your new GLE. |
| Executives and formal clients | Polite, last name | Mr. Harris, your GLC is ready. I will bring it to your office at 10 a.m. |
| New model launch | Confident, refined | The new GLC is here. Let me show you what makes it special. |
| Questions and complaints | Patient, empathetic | I understand. Let me look into this today and call you back with a clear answer. |

### Elevator Pitch

Hi, I am Mohsen, a Mercedes-Benz sales specialist with 20 years of experience. I make buying a luxury car one of your best experiences: everything disclosed up front, all the paperwork handled for you, and I am always one call away, even after delivery.

### Key Messages

1. Always available.
2. A relationship that continues after delivery.
3. Integrity: everything disclosed, no hidden costs.

### Word List

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Experience | Cheap |
| Tailored | Hurry |
| Disclosed, transparent | Deal of the year, last chance |
| Value | Pushy urgency of any kind |

## Visual Identity

### Logo

The logo is a wordmark in brushed gold on black. An oversized M forms the first letter of MOHSEN, and a gold car-silhouette swoosh sweeps through the M and over OHSEN. LUXURY AUTO sits below, between two thin gold lines. Logo file: `Mohsen_Luxury_Auto_Logo.png` (horizontal, 2:1).

- **Versions:** gold on black (primary, ready) and the M alone for small spaces like profile pictures and the favicon (ready). Still needed: a light version for white backgrounds and a one-color version for printing and engraving.
- **Placement:** top and center.
- **Never:** place it next to the Mercedes-Benz star, put it over busy photos, stretch it, or change its colors.

### Colors

The hex codes below were sampled from the logo file and are approximate. Confirm them with the designer's source file.

| Color | Role | Hex (approx.) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black | Primary background | #000000 |

| Gold | The logo, plus accents only: buttons, highlights, thin lines | #D9AE5F |

### Typography

| Use | Font | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headings | Classic serif, matching the logo (suggested: Cinzel) | Uppercase, generous letter spacing |
| Body text | Simple modern sans serif (suggested: Montserrat) | Regular weight, silver or white on black |

Both suggested fonts are free on Google Fonts.

### Imagery

Photos are **dark and cinematic**, like the logo: deep shadows, reflections on the paintwork, and warm highlights. Mohsen appears in delivery photos with clients, and the existing professional portraits are used for the website and profiles.

## Brand Applications

The website launches first, followed by Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook. The dealership name is optional on brand content.

### Website (suggested structure)

1. Hero: full logo, Expect the Best, and a Book a Test Drive button in gold
2. About Mohsen: story, professional portrait, and proof points
3. Why clients come back: the three key messages and client reviews
4. Specialties: GLC, GLE, AMG, EQ electric, Maybach, business and fleet
5. Contact: form, phone, email, and dealership address
