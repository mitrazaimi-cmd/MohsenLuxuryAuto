# Mohsen Luxury Auto — Logo Mega Prompt

**شرط‌ها:** حرف M بزرگ و طلایی، L و A کوچک‌تر و نقره‌ای، فونت سریف کلاسیک، قالب مربع ۱:۱ با لوگوی کامل.

**ابزار:** Muse Image، ChatGPT، Ideogram یا Gemini. کل متن داخل کادر زیر را کپی کنید.

```
=== ROLE ===
You are a world-class luxury brand identity designer who creates logos for high-end automotive, watch and jewelry brands. Design a final, production-ready logo.

=== BRAND ===
Brand name: Mohsen Luxury Auto
What it is: the personal brand of a Master Certified Mercedes-Benz sales specialist with 20 years of experience, based in Philadelphia, PA.
Slogan: Expect the Best
Brand keywords: Luxury, Dependability, Value
Personality: elegant, trustworthy, warm. Refined, never flashy.
Audience: high-income professionals and business owners.

=== SUBJECT: THE MONOGRAM (hero element, top of the logo) ===
- An interlocking "MLA" monogram in which the letter M is clearly dominant.
- The M is about twice the height of the L and A, and forms the main structure and silhouette of the whole mark.
- The M is polished brushed GOLD.
- The L and A are smaller, in brushed SILVER, elegantly nested inside the M or tucked against its right leg. They must never cover or cut through the M.
- A thin, sweeping gold swoosh arcs through the monogram, like the roofline of a luxury sports car.
- The M should be recognizable on its own as a standalone brand mark.

=== SECONDARY ELEMENT: CAR LINE ===
- Below the monogram: a single thin, elegant gold line drawing of a sleek luxury coupe silhouette, minimal and continuous, fading out at both ends.
- Generic car shape only. No real car model and no carmaker emblem.

=== TEXT (spell EXACTLY as written, centered, top to bottom) ===
1. "MOHSEN": the largest text, brushed silver, uppercase
2. "LUXURY AUTO": gold, smaller, uppercase, with a thin gold hairline on each side
3. "PREMIUM MERCEDES-BENZ SALES SPECIALIST": small, silver, uppercase
4. "PHILADELPHIA, PA": the smallest text, gold, uppercase, with thin gold lines to its left and right
No other words, letters or numbers anywhere in the image.

=== TYPOGRAPHY ===
- Monogram, "MOHSEN" and "LUXURY AUTO": a classic high-contrast serif with sharp serifs and dramatic thick-to-thin strokes, in the style of Cinzel or Trajan. Uppercase, generous letter spacing.
- The two small lines: a clean, modern geometric sans serif in the style of Montserrat, uppercase, very wide letter spacing.

=== COLOR PALETTE (strict) ===
- Background: pure black #000000
- Gold (the M, the swoosh, the car line, LUXURY AUTO, the location line): base #D9AE5F, highlights #F3D79A, shadows #A97F38
- Silver (L, A, MOHSEN, the descriptor): base #C9C9C9, highlights #EDEDED
- Use no other colors.

=== STYLE ===
Luxury emblem design. Realistic polished metal finishes with subtle brushed texture and soft reflections, crisp sharp edges, timeless and premium, like a high-end car badge or a fine jewelry brand. Clean and minimal. It must still read clearly when scaled down small.

=== LIGHTING ===
Soft, cinematic studio lighting from above. A gentle highlight glint on the gold M, and subtle reflections along the metal edges. Lighting stays on the logo only; the background stays pure flat black.

=== COMPOSITION ===
- Square format, perfectly centered, symmetrical and balanced.
- Vertical stack: monogram, then car line, then MOHSEN, then LUXURY AUTO, then the descriptor, then the location line.
- The monogram takes up about 40% of the logo's height.
- At least 10% empty black margin on every side.

=== FORMAT & QUALITY ===
Aspect ratio 1:1, 2048 x 2048 px, high resolution, sharp vector-like quality.

=== INTENDED USE ===
The primary brand logo for the website, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, email signature, business cards and gold-foil thank-you cards. It must work on screen and in print.

=== AVOID ===
The Mercedes-Benz three-pointed star or any other carmaker emblem or logo; misspelled or extra text; an L or A that overpowers the M; busy or textured backgrounds; neon; cartoon or clip-art style; heavy 3D bevels; drop shadows; glitter; lens flares; mockups, scenes or photos of real cars.

=== OUTPUT ===
Generate 4 variations that differ only in how the L and A are nested inside the M. Keep the colors, fonts and text identical across all four.
```

## نکته‌ها

- **محدودیت طول پرامپت:** اگر ابزار پرامپت بلند قبول نکرد، بخش‌های ROLE و INTENDED USE را حذف کنید. مهم‌ترین بخش‌ها SUBJECT، TEXT، COLOR و AVOID هستند.
- **تصویر مرجع:** اگر ابزار امکان آپلود عکس دارد، لوگوی فعلی را به‌عنوان مرجع آپلود کنید تا حس و حال کلی لوگو حفظ شود.
- **غلط املایی:** اگر متن‌های کوچک پایین اشتباه درآمد، لوگو را بدون آن دو خط بسازید و بعداً در Canva اضافه کنید.
- **بعد از نهایی شدن:** فایل لوگوی جدید را بفرستید تا صفحه‌ی لوگو در برند گاید به‌روز شود.
