Brand Story

Where the brand comes from, and the record behind it.

Mohsen has loved cars since childhood. The first time he sat behind the wheel of an S-Class, he knew he wanted to give that experience to others.

Twenty years later, he is a Master Certified Mercedes-Benz sales specialist at Mercedes-Benz of West Chester, known for patience, full disclosure and a relationship that continues long after the car is delivered.

Mission, Vision & Values

Why the brand exists, where it is going, and what it stands on.

Mission

To put customer satisfaction first by taking the time to show every client each car patiently, advising them on what truly fits their needs, and following up on every sale.

Vision

To grow Mohsen Luxury Auto into an independent brand, recognized by name, where more than 90 percent of clients are returning customers.

Values

II

CUSTOMER SATISFACTION

III

BEST VALUE

The client leaves knowing they got the most for their money.

Target Audience

Who the brand is for, and what they need to feel.

The core client is a high-income, well-educated professional or business owner, and business owners are the main group to grow.

Buyer Persona: The Business Owner

Primary persona
WhoHigh-income, well-educated professionals and business owners
BuysGLC and GLE, new and Certified Pre-Owned, mostly financed
Also servedAMG, EQ electric, Maybach, business and fleet purchases
Biggest worryThat something is not fully disclosed, or that there are hidden costs
Wants to feelSatisfied, with a great buying experience
Finds Mohsen throughReferrals and word of mouth
Answer the worry

Their biggest fear is hidden costs, so every message leads with full disclosure. Integrity is not a slogan here; it is the reason they buy.

Built for referrals

Because most new clients arrive by referral, every piece of content should be easy for a happy client to share.

02Verbal Identity

Voice & Tone

The brand sounds like an experienced friend, never a salesperson.

Brand Voice

I

ELEGANT

Refined, unhurried words. Short sentences. Nothing flashy or loud.

II

TRUSTWORTHY

Clear facts, everything disclosed, no exaggeration and no pressure.

III

WARM

Personal and attentive, like an experienced friend who is always one call away.

All brand content is written in English.

Tone by Situation

SituationToneExample
Social postsFriendly and shortDelivery day for Dr. Patel. Enjoy every mile of your new GLE.
Executives and formal clientsPolite, last nameMr. Harris, your GLC is ready. I will bring it to your office at 10 a.m.
New model launchConfident, refinedThe new GLC is here. Let me show you what makes it special.
Questions and complaintsPatient, empatheticI understand. Let me look into this today and call you back with a clear answer.

Hex codes were sampled from the logo file and are approximate; RGB and CMYK are converted from them. Confirm all three with the designer's source file. For print, ask the printer about a rich black.

Balance

Black · 70%
Silver · 22%
Gold

Recommended proportion. Black dominates, silver carries the words, gold is used sparingly so it stays special.

Do
  • Silver or white text on black
  • Gold for one button or highlight per view
  • Gold hairlines to divide and frame
Avoid
  • Large areas or long paragraphs in gold
  • Gold text on silver, or silver on white
  • Adding new brand colors
