Balance: about 70% black, 22% silver, 8% gold. Never large gold areas or long gold text. On gold buttons, the text is black (#000 on #D9AE5F ≈ 10:1).

## Typography

- Headings: **Cinzel** 400–600, uppercase, letter spacing 0.06–0.12em
- Body: **Montserrat** 300–500, 16px base on the web, line height 1.6–1.7
- Eyebrow / caption: Montserrat 500, 11–12px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.28em, gold
- Load with `next/font/google` (no layout shift)

## Spacing (spacious)

4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128 px. Section padding: 96px desktop, 64px mobile. Content max width: 1200px. Side gutter: 24px (mobile 20px).

## Components

- **Primary button:** gold background, black text, uppercase Montserrat 600, letter spacing 0.18em, 2px radius, min height 48px; hover lighter gold; visible focus ring (2px gold outline, 3px offset).
- **Secondary button:** transparent, 1px gold border, gold text.
- **Card:** surface background, 1px line border, optional 1px gold top hairline; hover: border turns gold-soft (no layout shift).
- **Input:** surface-2 background, 1px line border, 16px text, visible label above, error text below the field in `--danger`.
- **Section header:** gold eyebrow, Cinzel title, short 48px gold hairline.

## Motion

Fade-up 12px over 350ms, ease-out, on viewport enter. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, show everything in its final state. Hover transitions 200ms. Nothing fast or flashy.

One exception, the owner's decision: the homepage hero's animated logo (`components/hero-logo.tsx`) plays for everyone, reduced motion included. It plays once, about 5 seconds, then holds on the still logo. Windows reports reduced motion whenever its animation effects are turned off, which hid the logo animation from many laptop visitors.

## Page pattern

Hero (logo, slogan, CTA) → proof stats → key messages → services → testimonials and deliveries (social proof) → final booking CTA. A sticky header keeps "Book a Consultation" visible.

## Anti-patterns

Emoji icons · white page backgrounds · the Mercedes-Benz star · stretched or recolored logo · urgency wording ("last chance") · invisible focus states · placeholder-only labels.

One exception, the owner's decision (2026-10-20), made knowing the star rule and the dealership/trademark risk: the homepage "Why clients come back" photo (`public/images/why-clients-handshake.jpg`) shows the star on the car's grille and hood. Don't treat that photo as license for others.

## Pre-delivery checklist

- [ ] cursor: pointer on all clickable elements
- [ ] Hover transitions 150–300ms
- [ ] Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1

- [ ] prefers-reduced-motion respected
- [ ] Responsive at 375, 768, 1024, 1440 px; no horizontal scroll
- [ ] No content hidden behind the fixed header
